# Weekly metrics: week of Nov 2

Activation rose to 42% this week, up from 35.1%. The new onboarding checklist caused the lift.

## Numbers

| Metric | Oct 26 | Nov 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Activation rate | 35.1% | 38.4% |
| New teams | 1,187 | 1,240 |
| Weekly active teams | 9,820 | 10,115 |

## What happened

- The onboarding checklist reached 100% of new orgs on Monday.
- Churn fell for the third straight week.
- Weekly active teams passed 10,000.

## Next

- A 10% holdout for the checklist starts Nov 10, so we can tell whether the the checklist moved activation.
