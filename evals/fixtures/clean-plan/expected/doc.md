# Saved searches: rollout plan

Owner: Sam Rivera · Status: approved Nov 10

Saved searches go to 10% of students on Thursday Nov 12. If every metric below stays healthy for that week, they go to everyone on Friday Nov 20. If any metric lands between healthy and pause, we stay at 10% and Sam decides.

## What we watch

| Metric | Healthy | Pause if |
|---|---|---|
| Students in the 10% who save at least one search | 8% or more | under 4% |
| Search error rate | no change | up more than 1 percentage point |
| Support tickets about search | under 5 a day | 20 or more in a day |

Marco checks these each morning of the 10% week and posts them in #saved-searches.

## If we pause

Sam or Marco can call a pause. Marco turns off the `saved_searches` flag and tells Lee Park in support. Saved searches stay in the database (Marco confirmed this Nov 9), so nothing is lost when the flag comes back on. Sam decides when it does.
