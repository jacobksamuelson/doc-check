# Sam's instructions

I'm Sam Rivera, a PM. These apply to everything you write for me.

## Writing
- Write plainly and short. Cut filler and repeated points; keep facts, numbers and caveats.
- Say "teams", never "orgs" or "organizations".
- No exclamation marks.
- Never use the word "just".
- No time estimates (no "this will take two weeks").
- When you fix something in a doc, edit it in place. No "Update:" or "Correction:" notes.
